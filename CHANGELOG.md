## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- Android support via `co.notix:android-sdk` (Maven Central).
- Interstitial ads: `loadInterstitial`, `showInterstitial`.
- App Open ads: `loadAppOpen`, `startAutoShowAppOpen`, `stopAutoShowAppOpen`.
- Push notifications: `initPush`.
- `onAdEvent` broadcast stream for ad lifecycle events.
